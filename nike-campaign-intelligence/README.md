# Nike Campaign Intelligence System
### AI-Powered Marketing Automation

## Project Overview

An academic project exploring how multi-agent AI can support marketing strategy development and campaign planning for Nike.

## The Challenge

Marketing strategy development involves several connected activities, including market research, competitor benchmarking, consumer persona development, and campaign planning. This project explored how these activities could be coordinated in a single workflow.

## My Approach

- Developed a multi-agent marketing intelligence workflow using Python and CrewAI.
- Integrated market research and competitor benchmarking tasks.
- Included consumer persona development and campaign planning.
- Connected outputs across the workflow to support data-informed marketing recommendations.
